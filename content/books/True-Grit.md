Possibly the two biggest compliments I can give to this book are these:

Firstly, as soon as I had finished it I gave it to a friend the very next day. I wish for someone else to read and enjoy it as I had.

Also on the next day, I ordered a book by Donna Tartt - who provided the introduction - so taken with her words about it was I.

True Grit is a gritty and brutal western that follows Mattie Ross as she seeks help finding the man who killed her father. She enlists the surly marshal Rooster Cogburn in her search. The book is tense and violent, funny and atmospheric. It really had me wondering what was next and if they'd ever find their man.

I believe it's the writing of the book that truly shines. Portis writes from Mattie's perspective, and does not use any contractions, it really feels like a true old west diary.

I did not know I liked Western stories, but this is up there with my all-time favourite books. And short, too.
