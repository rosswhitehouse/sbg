When it comes to books that I have learned from, there can be no greater value proposition than Judith Butler. This book is at the same time informative, entertaining and thoroughly heartbreaking, as Butler breaks down the history of the ‘gender ideology’ - a theory weaponised by Christianity and the far right to enforce conservative values and oppress people who are queer by gender or sexuality.

I feel that as a total novice in learning about gender theory, this book has pretty much everything you need to start off. It discusses the gender criticalness of governments across the world, the TERF movement, Trump and the Supreme Court, sex vs gender, intersectionality, colonialism and so much more. I really believe that there can be no better primer for reading more about these things.

One of the biggest takeaways was all of the hypocrisy in the arguments of the right against gender. They say - for example - that queer people are a predatory danger, when in fact queer people are far more likely to be aware of and uphold consent and conversations about bodily autonomy than straight men certainly are. Which tallies with my experience exactly.

I have truly never thought about or talked about a book so much in my life. Butler got right under my skin and made me think about the subject more than I ever have before. It's a truly remarkable and powerful book and I'm so glad to have learned from it.
