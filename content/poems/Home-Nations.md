# DRAFT 1
The Home Nations will secede from the union.

Land stolen from their mothers. Castles burned to the ground. The very language of the soil evicted.

And why not?

Let the fat gammon faces reform Jolly Old England right into the sea and let her sink.

And long live the new union. The island nations of Éire, Alba and Cymru.

And if England should survive let her repent and make amends. Let her be a giver and not a taker.

Reparate to the colonies. To India and Pakistan, Africa, the Caribbean, the indigenous peoples of North America and Oceania.

Make amends on bended knee for a thousand years of a thousand atrocities.

Do not let us forget our solemn duty.

For England, Home and Beauty.
