The news came today that the First Ministers of Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland are organising to persuade PM Andy Burnham to give them independence from the United Kingdom.

I'm sure I needn't go into the generations of oppression and down-treading. Land has been stolen from their mothers. Their castles burned to the ground. Their very languages evicted. I'd be upset myself.

And for me, I say let them go if they wish to go. I have a very limited understanding of the complicated political and economical ties to the United Kingdom. We are, presumably, stronger together, but I also see the union being weighed down by many problems that England isn't dealing with. In Scotland, for example, perscriptions are free. As is education. These are the decisions of a government trying to do something for it's people. And England has come nowhere near this.

England slides nearer to fascist oblivion with the chancers of the Reform party taking illegal investments to fund their racist policies and propaganda. I understand Reform are a problem in Wales as well, perhaps a little distance will be healthy for the Welsh.

So let the fat gammon faces of the Reform party sink jolly old England into the sea. And long live the island nations of Ireland, Scotland and Wales.

As for England itself, I hope this is a wake-up call for Whitehall that this is the final nail in the coffin of the old empire. The rot has well and truly set in. My dream is that they approach the table softly, give the home nations their freedom, and look to other ways we can make amends for generational wrong-doing of our fathers. I'm talking your friend and mine: reparations. Look to Africa, India, Pakistan, Argentina, even Canada and Australia, where native populations were driven from their homes.
